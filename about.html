GET TO KNOW WICKETRA

ABOUT WICKETRA.

WICKETRA is a cricket tournament management platform being developed to bring tournament organizers, teams and players together in one organized place. Our goal is to simplify the way cricket tournaments are created, managed and followed.

01 / OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

Cricket tournaments involve many activities, including team registration, player management, match scheduling, scoring and tournament results.

Managing these activities separately can become difficult. WICKETRA aims to bring these processes together through a structured platform designed around the needs of cricket tournaments.

02 / WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

One Platform. Every Match.

WICKETRA is being developed around the essential activities involved in organizing and following cricket competitions.

01

Tournament Management

Create and organize cricket tournaments with different competition formats, registration settings and tournament information.

02

Team & Player Management

Organize team registrations, player details and participation throughout a tournament.

03

Fixtures & Scheduling

Organize tournament fixtures and match schedules according to the selected competition format.

04

Live Scores & Statistics

Build a structured match centre for scoring, match results, points tables and player statistics.

03 / OUR VISION

THE FUTURE OF CRICKET MANAGEMENT.

We envision a cricket community where managing tournaments is more organized, teams can participate with confidence and players and fans can follow the game more conveniently.
