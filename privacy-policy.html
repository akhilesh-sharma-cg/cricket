PRIVACY POLICY.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WICKETRA ("we", "our" or "us") handles information when you visit or use our website. It describes the types of information that may be collected, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to WICKETRA and its website features. Actual data collection depends on which features and third-party services are currently enabled and how visitors use the website.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features available and the information you choose to provide, we may collect:

  • Account information: Name, email address and other details provided when creating or managing an account.
  • Cricket information: Team names, player details, tournament registrations, fixtures, match scores and results submitted by users.
  • Communication information: Messages, feedback, support requests and other information you send to us.
  • Technical information: Browser type, device information, IP address and website usage information, where collected by the website or its service providers.
  • Cookies and preferences: Information stored through cookies or similar technologies, where these are used.

Please do not submit passwords, financial account details or other sensitive personal information through general contact messages or public website fields.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Operating and maintaining the WICKETRA website.
  • Supporting account and tournament management.
  • Managing team and player registrations.
  • Displaying fixtures, scores and tournament results.
  • Responding to questions and support requests.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Improving website functionality and performance.
  • Meeting applicable legal requirements.

3. Publicly Visible Information

Certain information submitted to WICKETRA may be displayed publicly when the relevant feature is available. This may include tournament details, team names, player profiles, fixtures, match results and statistics.

Users should avoid submitting personal information that they do not want displayed publicly. Organizers should ensure they have the appropriate permission or lawful basis before submitting information about other people, including players and team members.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

WICKETRA or third-party services integrated into the website may use cookies and similar technologies. Depending on the service, these technologies may help maintain website functionality, remember preferences, understand website usage or support advertising.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to display, measure or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable laws, provider policies and available user settings.

For information about Google's advertising practices and available controls, visit: Google's Advertising Privacy and Terms .

5. Third-Party Services

WICKETRA may use external service providers to support website operations. Depending on the features enabled, these may include:

  • Website hosting and deployment providers.
  • Database and data storage providers.
  • Authentication and account management providers.
  • Website analytics providers.
  • Advertising providers.

These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

We do not claim that every service listed above is currently active. The services used depend on the website's actual configuration.

6. How Information May Be Shared

We do not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary:

  • To provide features requested by users.
  • With service providers supporting website operations.
  • To comply with applicable laws or legal requests.
  • To protect the security, rights and safety of users or WICKETRA.
  • To investigate suspected fraud, abuse or unauthorized activity.

Information that users or organizers make publicly available may be viewed by other visitors and may be copied or shared outside the website.

7. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss or misuse.

However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their account credentials and contact us if they suspect unauthorized account activity.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant services, maintain records, resolve disputes, protect the platform or comply with legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the purpose of processing and applicable legal requirements.

9. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law and the features available, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about personal data held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Raise concerns about how your information is handled.
  • Manage cookies through your browser settings.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address provided in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Children's Privacy

WICKETRA does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in a manner prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be considered.

11. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. We do not control the privacy practices, content or security of those external services.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any external website you visit.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website features, service providers or legal requirements change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.
